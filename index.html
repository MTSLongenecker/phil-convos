Featured Philosophers

Derek Parfit

1942–2017

Personal Identity, Rationality, Ethics

British philosopher known for his rigorous analytical style and exploration of what makes us the same person over time.

Key Concepts

  • Personal identity continuity
  • Psychological vs physical identity
  • Teletransporter thought experiment
  • Psychological continuity criterion

Socrates

c. 470–399 BCE

Philosophical Method, Ethics, Logic

Ancient Greek philosopher known for his Socratic Method of questioning, dialectical approach, and pursuit of truth through dialogue.

Key Concepts

  • Socratic Method of questioning
  • Dialectic and philosophical dialogue
  • Virtue ethics and moral excellence
  • Knowledge vs belief distinction

David Lewis

1941–2001

Modal Realism, Counterpart Theory

American philosopher known for modal realism, counterpart theory, and Humean supervenience.

Key Concepts

  • Modal realism: all possible worlds are real
  • Counterpart theory for trans-world identity
  • Humean supervenience
  • Conventions and language meaning

About Phil-Convos

Phil-Convos is designed to make philosophical exploration accessible and engaging. By creating AI representations of philosophers, we aim to:

  • Provide interactive learning experiences
  • Help users understand complex philosophical concepts
  • Foster critical thinking and self-reflection
  • Create a platform for philosophical dialogue and exploration

The project is open-source and welcomes contributions from philosophers, educators, and developers.
